Update: Finland says Ukraine “likely” hit Sparta II; Russian helicopter breaches Finnish airspace

Lede. The Finnish Defence Forces assessed that Ukraine was “likely” behind the drone attack on the Russian cargo ship Sparta II, which caught fire and drifted into Finnish territorial waters (https://x.com/i/status/2124167197165797618; Kyiv Independent https://x.com/i/status/2124197559719940511).

The Kyiv Independent describes the ship as sanctioned by the US and Ukraine and linked to the Russian military.

Context. Finland’s Border Guard is running the rescue and evacuating the crew and is preparing for possible environmental damage (https://x.com/i/status/2124148828051845623). Separately, the Defence Ministry reported a Russian helicopter 4.1 km inside Finnish airspace off Loviisa (https://x.com/i/status/2124149633996017914); a Finnish Border Guard post said a helicopter flew to the ship without contacting it (same thread).

Why it matters for Europe. A Ukrainian deep strike now has direct consequences for an EU and NATO member’s waters, airspace and environment; Helsinki has to manage both the Russian reaction and a possible oil spill.

What to watch. Whether the ship sinks; Russian retaliation claims or naval escorts for Kaliningrad traffic; Finnish and Estonian statements; Kyiv’s response.

Kaliningrad drone alert; Estonia to close its Russian border crossings

Lede. Kaliningrad’s governor declared a region-wide drone alert after an object was reportedly detected approaching from Lithuania, then said there was no threat; Khrabrovo airport reopened after about 20 minutes (https://x.com/i/status/2124196519511208090, https://x.com/i/status/2124198045185118528).

The source chain runs through TASS, so this is a Russian official claim.

Context. @ClashReport reports that Estonia will close all five border checkpoints with Russia on 1 December, after Tallinn said Russian military intelligence ordered an attempted arson attack at a defence facility near the capital (https://x.com/i/status/2124161193531101455). The original Estonian statement was not checked.

Why it matters for Europe. Russian claims of threats “from Lithuania” can serve as a pretext narrative. A full Estonian border closure would be the toughest Baltic step yet against Russian sabotage.

What to watch. Lithuanian response; official Estonian government confirmation and the scope of the closure.

Iran: contested F-16 claim and new IRGC drone claim; CENTCOM denies

Lede. Iran’s army said its air defences hit a US F-16 over the Gulf on Friday evening (Reuters via Al Jazeera, citing Iranian state media, https://x.com/i/status/2124142636852535755); CENTCOM denied any attack (https://x.com/i/status/2124152485350723725).

The IRGC later claimed it downed a US reconnaissance drone near Bushehr (https://x.com/i/status/2124145592427307518).

Context. Deputy FM Kazem Gharibabadi called the US blockade an “act of war” (https://x.com/i/status/2124155269751923023); the Foreign Minister said reopening Hormuz “is not difficult” if Washington accepts Iran’s “reasonable conditions” (https://x.com/i/status/2124173334955118746). A Qatari delegation held two hours of talks in Tehran (https://x.com/i/status/2124148647403069595). Trump told Fox News he will make a “permanent” deal ensuring Iran does not get a nuclear weapon (https://x.com/i/status/2124161098886652098; AJ https://x.com/i/status/2124201161641394620). IRGC commander Ahmad Vahidi visited an underground missile facility (AJ, https://x.com/i/status/2124191017146654739). Reuters: UKMTO reported two tanker incidents near Oman and Iran says one vessel hit a mine east of Hormuz (https://x.com/i/status/2124148381127762190).

Why it matters for Europe. Energy prices and shipping insurance for Europe depend on Hormuz; mixed signals — information war about the F-16, Qatari mediation and Trump’s “deal” talk — make escalation hard to read.

What to watch. Further mine or tanker incidents; any announced Qatar-brokered framework; US strike decisions.

US air assets flow east: F-35B to Lajes

Lede. Ten F-35B from VMFA-214 with KC-46A tankers are arriving at Lajes in the Azores on the second leg of “Coronet East 008”, which the tracker links to the Iran war (https://x.com/i/status/2124195442632044662, earlier leg https://x.com/i/status/2124128290499617053).

Why it matters for Europe. Portuguese and other European bases remain staging points for US Middle East operations, a political exposure for European governments.

What to watch. Onward destination (likely via Spain/Italy or the UK); further tanker bridges.

Yemen–Saudi war: Sanaa threatens Saudi solar complex

Lede. A Houthi military source in Sanaa threatened to target the Shuaiba 2 solar complex, described as Saudi Arabia’s largest, in retaliation for a strike on a solar plant in Mokha (Arabic-language relay, unverified, https://x.com/i/status/2124185594561798343).

Context. Houthi drone strikes on Saudi-backed PLC positions and tanks were reported (https://x.com/i/status/2124150379852026333); NASA FIRMS showed a fire at the port of Jizan on 21 November (https://x.com/i/status/2124152976285618573).

Why it matters for Europe. Attacks on Saudi energy infrastructure add to Red Sea and Gulf supply risk alongside Hormuz.

What to watch. Any strike on Saudi energy sites; coalition air strikes in Yemen.

Horn of Africa: UAE reportedly deploys Chinese CH-95 drones in Puntland

Lede. A post citing the New York Times says the UAE has deployed Chinese-origin CH-95 combat drones at Bosaso airport in Puntland, Somalia (https://x.com/i/status/2124129427109925308).

The NYT original was not checked.

Why it matters for Europe. Chinese armed drones spreading through Gulf partners into the Gulf of Aden area, where EU naval operations Atalanta and Aspides operate.

What to watch. Confirmation from the NYT original; drone use over Yemen or Sudan.
