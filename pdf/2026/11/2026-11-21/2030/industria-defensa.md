## US Senate Democrats to investigate the cuts to Ukraine's Starshield access

**Senator Chris Coons said in Halifax on 20 November that one of Congress's first tasks next year will be to look into what crippled Ukraine's access to SpaceX's Starshield, the military variant of Starlink.**

"This is not Elon Musk's personal toy," he said. Ukrainian officials blamed a sudden network change in October for drone and air-defence failures; Kyiv's chargé d'affaires in Washington said on 9 November that speed limits were again in place along the contact line, with no explanation from the Pentagon or SpaceX (Breaking Defense, 21 Nov).

Source: https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/senate-gearing-up-to-investigate-ukraines-access-to-starshield/

**Why it matters for Europe.** Europe's support for Ukraine's drone war depends on a US company's satellite network that can be throttled without warning. It strengthens the case for speeding up the EU's IRIS² constellation and Eutelsat OneWeb as European alternatives. *CEAFE:* A clear example of how technological dependence becomes operational vulnerability.

## Update: Canada says it has seen no change after Hegseth's memo

**Canada's Defence Minister David McGuinty said at the Halifax International Security Forum that Ottawa has not seen the classified document and that defence, security and intelligence cooperation continues.**

Pentagon spokesman Sean Parnell said the department is "taking steps to ensure its engagements with Canada advance tangible US defense objectives" (Breaking Defense; Reuters on X). This follows the 14:30 lead (Hegseth's memo and the Canada–Germany–Norway naval MoU).

Sources: https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/canada-hasnt-seen-changes-to-defense-relationship-with-us-says-mcguinty/ · https://x.com/Reuters/status/2124183601218474345

**Why it matters for Europe.** Even NATO's closest US neighbour is being told cooperation must serve "US defense objectives". This is a reason for Canada to look to European partners, as it is doing with Germany and Norway.

## China's defence minister holds talks in London, the first such visit in 26 years

**Admiral Dong Jun met UK Defence Secretary Wes Streeting in London on 19 November.**

China's Ministry of National Defense says both sides agreed to promote "steady, sound and sustainable" military-to-military relations. Anadolu reports London says it will stay alert to espionage and cyberattacks while engaging.

Sources: http://eng.mod.gov.cn/2025xb/N/T/16501058.html · https://www.aa.com.tr/en/asia-pacific/chinese-british-defense-chiefs-hold-1st-talks-in-uk-in-26-years/4133040 · https://x.com/anadoluagency/status/2124189889885335885

**Why it matters for Europe.** Dialogue with Beijing on its own terms is a sign of strategic autonomy rather than the exclusive bloc-building Washington promotes. *CEAFE:* Europeans gain from talking to every global power without asking permission.

## Lockheed Martin markets "Joint Fires": HIMARS, GMLRS and F-35 linked in one network

**Lockheed Martin promoted a networked fires concept linking HIMARS launchers, GMLRS rockets and the F-35 to share data and re-target missiles in flight (Lockheed Martin on X).**

It is a sales pitch aimed at buyers of all three, many of them European.

Source: https://x.com/LockheedMartin/status/2124118149062676493

**Why it matters for Europe.** Each added link ties buyers deeper into a US system-of-systems. Europe's own long-range strike effort is the ELSA initiative. *CEAFE:* Every networked package bought off the US shelf makes the next European choice harder.
